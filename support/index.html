Drift Remote Support

Drift turns your iPhone or iPad into a Bluetooth remote, with a trackpad and Back, Home and Play/Pause, for another iPhone or iPad you choose. There is no receiver app to install.

Connecting

  1. Keep Drift open on the device you hold.
  2. Wake and unlock the iPhone or iPad you want to control. Drift may connect automatically.
  3. If it doesn’t, open Settings › Bluetooth on that device and choose Drift Remote.

To control an iPhone, first turn on Settings › Accessibility › Touch › AssistiveTouch on that iPhone.

Still not connecting?

Wake the other device and reopen Drift. If it still doesn’t connect, forget Drift Remote in that device’s Bluetooth settings, turn Bluetooth off and on, then try again. In Drift, Connection Help (the question mark) has the same steps, plus What Works, a Test Report and Diagnostics you can share if you want help.

Good to know

  • Drift works only while it is open on your device.
  • Drift has no account, no analytics and no app-operated server. See the privacy policy.
