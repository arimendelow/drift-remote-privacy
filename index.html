Drift Remote Privacy

Drift is a simple Bluetooth remote. Privacy is part of that simplicity.

The Drift app does not collect, store off-device, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Drift has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, app-operated backend, or third-party SDKs.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used locally only to make the controller appear as an input device to another device you choose. Drift cannot read content or activity from that target.

On-device preferences

Drift stores onboarding completion, selected target type, whether this installation has ever connected to a target, and your compatibility-result choices on this device. The connection flag is used only to choose first-pair versus reconnect guidance. Deleting Drift deletes these preferences.

Voluntary beta feedback

If you send TestFlight feedback or email, Apple or your email provider transmits that message to the developer outside the Drift app. It may contain details you choose to provide, including controller and target model, OS version, video app, adapter and TV, control results, reconnect behavior, screenshots, contact address, and message content.

Only the developer can access this feedback. It is used solely to diagnose Drift and assess compatibility, retained only as long as needed for that purpose, then deleted. You may request deletion at ari@mendelow.me.

TestFlight and its feedback transport are provided by Apple and subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. Email is subject to your and the developer’s email providers.

Contact

For privacy questions or feedback-deletion requests, email ari@mendelow.me.
